## Artwork and staged construction

Each of the 50 planned buildings requires five independently drawn, detailed textured isometric block-art stages: foundation, frame, walls, roof, then a finished inhabited or lit building. Plot is the zero-progress ground marker. Every stage shares the same ground footprint, camera angle and lighting so timber, masonry and roof connect cleanly. Stages must add visible structural material; no opacity-only fades or cuts from a finished image. Distinct silhouettes, readable scale on Mac laptops, depth ordering and clear gaps around roads are acceptance requirements. One bakery stage set is implemented first for review. Every other future building remains an explicit asset requirement, not a substitute cloned from the old five buildings.

Per district, five buildings share 25 fixed construction milestones. Independent learning evidence advances one milestone every 4 percentage points, with five interleaved rounds: foundations by 20%, frames by 40%, walls by 60%, roofs by 80%, inhabited buildings by 100%. The next milestone identifies its building and exact stage. This allocation reuses existing villageLevels, and full verified mastery reaches full construction with no extra currency or unrelated gate.

Current learning percent comes from that wave’s frozen reviewed learning contract. Architecture already earned follows its own saved highPercent; mistakes indicate repairs separately and do not erase finished architecture. Starting another wave changes no earlier denominator. Legacy Wave 1 displays only actually purchased buildings and makes no new mastery or date claim.

## Activation acceptance for every future wave

Before any planned district becomes an active lesson, receive the teacher vocabulary and test requirements, validate and freeze its reviewed learning contract, and explicitly bind that lesson identity to the district. Imports never bind by their position, title, or a guessed wave number. The current implementation reserves future catalog waveId as null; unknown imports retain a neutral lesson outpost and never consume these themed plots.

Before activation, all five building stage sets and district landmark must pass visual review at 0, 4, 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100%. Verify frozen target evidence, honest repeated-answer behavior, completion equivalence, prior-wave independence, earned-architecture repairs, old-wave navigation and exact unfinished-question/correction resume. Confirm roads, bridges, landmarks and foundation previews remain readable at laptop widths without overlap, floating roofs, clipping or invisible changes. A completion celebration can use restrained light and motion, with no timer or farming obligation.
