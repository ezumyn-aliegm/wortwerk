## Prompt set

### Bakery stage atlas

Production game sprite sheet for Wortwerk. Detailed textured Minecraft-inspired isometric block-art bakery stages, not a diagram. One wide transparent PNG atlas with six equal-width cells in one horizontal row. Each cell has the same orthographic isometric camera, footprint and ground contact, centered and contained. Left to right, empty dirt marked plot with corner stakes, stone foundation only, timber frame with posts and beams and no walls or roof, stone and timber walls with open top and no roof, complete blue tiled roof and chimney without business decorations, then inhabited cozy bakery with warm windows, striped awning, bread display, flowers and baker. Same building grows cumulatively. No floating roofs, cropped buildings, fades, text or scenery. Detailed stone joints, timber planks and roof tiles. Reference supplies style only. Transparent background.

### Connected terrain

Production terrain background for a connected growing settlement. Detailed textured isometric voxel block art with one continuous terraced grassy landmass, winding stone roads, stream and stone bridges, autumn trees and farm scenery, sea and wooden dock, evergreen forest and distant mountain gateway silhouette. No buildings on the clearings, text, cards or district outlines. Twelve empty grass or dirt clearing areas in a four-column, three-row grid, with connected paths. Scenery between clearings. Wide 3:2 view. Reference supplies style only. Original requested centers were approximate. Rendered anchors were corrected to the actual ground surfaces after browser review.

### Future landmark atlas

Ten isolated detailed isometric finished landmark sprites on transparent background, five equal columns by two equal rows. Entire sprites contained with generous margins, same camera and scale. Top row, bakery, lighthouse, forge, treehouse and alpine lodge. Bottom row, watermill, glass conservatory, observatory, festival pavilion and grand castle gateway. Different silhouettes, block textures, warm lanterns and small ground bases. No text or scene background. Reference supplies style only. These are planning landmarks, not current earned architecture.

### Terrain correction

Edit only two areas of the first terrain. Preserve camera, paths, other clearings, lighting and textures. Replace the completed distant castle gateway with a natural rocky forested cliff. Expand the bottom-right shore into a broad empty stone quay suitable for a future gateway overlay at x86%, y62%. Connect it to the land with stone road and retain wooden dock below. No building on the platform, text or labels. Preserve 1536-by-1024 size and block-art style. This targeted fourth call addressed the independent review's floating-quay and prebuilt-gateway findings.

## Visual limits

Future landmarks are reviewed concept destinations. The remaining 49 future buildings still require genuine staged assets before their districts can become playable. Vocabulary, tests and dates are also required before activation. The connected preview never creates those assignments. The existing Wave 2 upgrade art retains its accepted style, rather than claiming to be new foundation/frame art.

At small phone widths, the map uses a contained horizontal scroll to retain readable buildings. Mac laptop layouts fit the map. Large selected previews expose future artwork in detail. No free camera or camera animation is introduced.
